About Amy

Amy Shojai combines her passion for animals with her expertise in behavior consulting and creative writing to produce authoritative, accessible pet care content. As a Certified Animal Behavior Consultant, she provides science-based insights into dog and cat behavior, helping pet owners build stronger, healthier relationships with their companions.

With a diverse background that includes traditional publishing, self-publishing, radio hosting, and television appearances, Amy brings a unique perspective to pet care education. She understands both the science of animal behavior and the art of communicating complex topics in ways that resonate with everyday pet parents.

Media & Speaking

Amy is a sought-after spokesperson for the pet products industry and a frequent guest expert on national media. Her experience includes:

  • Host of the "Pet Peeves" podcast on PetLifeRadio.com
  • Expert contributor to Animal Planet's DOGS101 and CATS101
