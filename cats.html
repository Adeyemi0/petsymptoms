Cat Care FAQs

Quick answers to common questions cat owners ask

What is the best cat breed for apartments?+
Breeds like the Russian Blue, British Shorthair, and Ragdoll are often recommended for apartments due to their calm temperament and lower exercise needs. However, any cat can thrive in an apartment with adequate vertical space, toys, and attention.
How often should cats visit the vet?+
Adult cats should have a wellness exam at least once a year. Kittens need more frequent visits for vaccinations and checkups, typically every 3 to 4 weeks until they are about 16 weeks old. Senior cats (7+ years) benefit from twice yearly visits.
What are signs of illness in cats?+
Cats often hide illness, so look for subtle changes like hiding more than usual, changes in litter box habits, reduced grooming, lethargy, changes in appetite, vomiting, or vocalizing differently. Any persistent change warrants a veterinary consultation.
How do I introduce a new cat to my home?+
Start by keeping the new cat in a separate room with food, water, and litter. Swap scents using blankets, then allow visual contact through a barrier before supervised face-to-face meetings. Go slowly and reward calm behavior.
