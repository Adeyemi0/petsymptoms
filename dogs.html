Dog Care FAQs

Quick answers to common questions dog owners ask

What is the best dog breed for first time owners?+
Breeds like Labrador Retrievers, Golden Retrievers, and Cavalier King Charles Spaniels are often recommended for first time owners due to their friendly temperament, trainability, and adaptability. However, the best breed depends on your lifestyle, living situation, and activity level.
How often should I take my dog to the vet?+
Adult dogs should have a wellness exam at least once a year. Puppies need more frequent visits for vaccinations and checkups, typically every 3 to 4 weeks until they are about 16 weeks old. Senior dogs may benefit from twice yearly visits.
What are common signs of illness in dogs?+
Common signs include changes in appetite or water consumption, lethargy, vomiting, diarrhea, coughing, difficulty breathing, limping, excessive scratching, behavioral changes, and unexplained weight loss.
How much exercise does my dog need?+
Exercise needs vary by breed, age, and health. Most adult dogs benefit from 30 minutes to 2 hours of activity daily. High energy breeds like Border Collies need more, while lower energy breeds may be satisfied with shorter walks.
