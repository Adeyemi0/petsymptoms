Exotic Pet Care FAQs

Quick answers to common questions exotic pet owners ask

Are reptiles good pets for beginners?+
Yes, several reptile species are well suited for beginners. Leopard geckos, corn snakes, and crested geckos have manageable care requirements, calm temperaments, and do not require daily handling. Proper research and habitat setup are essential before bringing any exotic pet home.
What temperature and lighting do reptiles need?+
Most reptiles require a temperature gradient in their enclosure with a warm basking spot and a cooler area. UVB lighting is essential for many species to synthesize vitamin D3 and metabolize calcium properly. Specific requirements vary significantly by species, so consult a care sheet or exotic veterinarian.
How can I tell if my exotic pet is sick?+
Signs of illness in exotic pets often include loss of appetite, lethargy, abnormal shedding, swelling, discharge from eyes or nose, changes in droppings, or hiding more than usual. Exotic animals can decline quickly, so prompt evaluation by a veterinarian experienced with reptiles or small mammals is critical.
What do pet reptiles eat?+
Diet varies widely by species. Insectivores like geckos eat crickets, dubia roaches, and mealworms dusted with calcium. Herbivores like bearded dragons require leafy greens and vegetables. Carnivores like snakes eat appropriately sized frozen thawed rodents. Always research species specific dietary needs.
